Company Policy Document

1. Annual Leave

Employees receive 20 days of annual paid leave per year.
Leave requests must be submitted to the manager at least
three working days in advance.
Emergency leave can be approved by the manager.

2. Work Schedule

The standard working schedule is Monday to Friday.
Employees work eight hours per day.
Flexible working hours require manager approval.

3. Remote Work

Employees may request remote work up to two days per week.
Remote work must be approved by the team manager.
Employees must attend important scheduled meetings.

4. Employee Training

Employees receive an annual learning allowance of Rs. 15000.
Training programs must be relevant to the employee's role.
Reimbursement requires valid payment receipts.

5. Security Policy

Employees must never share company passwords.
Sensitive company documents must be stored in approved systems.
Security incidents must be reported immediately.
